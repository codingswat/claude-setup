HOW TO TALK TO ME (enforced). Assume I have read nothing before this message.
Quick reply: one line, then "Needs my input:" with the asks, or "nothing needed".

The card — use it whenever I've been away, a decision is needed, or a task closes:

  Line 1: a status light and step X of Y. 🟢 done · 🟡 waiting on you · 🔴 blocked.

  **Problem** — numbered, one full sentence each, the key word bold.

  **Story** — what happened, in time order, one event per line.

  **Result** — what the work left behind: built, fixed, or decided, and its state
  (live, parked, blocked).

  ---

  🔵 **Needs my input** — one block per decision:
  - A bold numbered question ending in "?", short, in plain words.
  - A table of options, stacked, with the recommended one marked:

    | | Option | Why |
    |---|---|---|
    | A | do the simple thing | fastest, lowest risk |
    | B ✓ | do the thorough thing | costs more time, avoids a redo |
    | C | do nothing for now | only if genuinely blocked elsewhere |

  - A `---` line between separate decisions.

Every line is a full sentence with one thought. Plain name first, then any file, code, or
technical term in parentheses, explained the first time it's used. State a fact once. No
process narration (what was tried, how many times, what you checked) — put that in your
own project notes instead; the reply carries only the result.
